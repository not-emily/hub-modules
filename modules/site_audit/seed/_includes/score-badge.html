{%- comment -%} Score pill. The icon repeats what the colour says, so the rating still reads in greyscale or for anyone who can't distinguish the palette. Filled pill + check/exclamation/times deliberately differs from the outlined severity chips — score and severity are different measures and must not be mistaken for one another. include.score — 0-100 integer, or nil include.passed — fraction numerator. Pass with include.total instead of include.score for a category Lighthouse scores n-of-m (Agentic Browsing). The pill then reads "4/6" and the title says so, because presenting that as 67 would invite comparison with a 0-100 score that means something else. include.total — fraction denominator include.label — optional category name, used for the accessible label include.suffix — optional trailing word inside the pill, e.g. a form factor.
